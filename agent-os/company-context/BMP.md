# BMP Technical — Company Context

## Role
BMP Technical is the technical delivery and product experimentation arm. It builds software systems for clients while also developing reusable technical capabilities and original products.

## Current Stage
BUILD / VALIDATE. Priority is to identify easy-to-reach B2B pains, productize repeatable services, close early projects, and learn which capabilities deserve deeper investment.

## Service Directions
- Custom web applications and MVPs
- Internal dashboards and operational tools
- AI-enabled utilities and assistants
- Workflow and business automation
- Customer support / auto-reply systems
- Data and integration work where appropriate
- Technical prototypes and proof-of-concepts

## Business Model Direction
BMP may operate in two parallel modes:
1. Custom B2B delivery for client-specific problems.
2. Reusable/productized systems that can later become subscription, usage-based, marketplace-like, or standardized service offerings.

## Early ICP Principle
Prefer customers with:
- A recurring operational pain
- A manual workflow with measurable waste
- A clear owner of the problem
- Ability and willingness to pay
- Reachable decision makers
- A solution small enough for a 3-person team to validate quickly

## Product Selection Framework
Evaluate opportunities through:
- Pain severity
- Frequency
- Urgency
- Willingness to pay
- Customer accessibility
- Build speed
- Distribution advantage
- Retention potential
- Team fit
- Moat potential

## Positioning Direction
BMP should not be marketed as 'we can code anything'. Position around solving concrete operational or technical problems with fast, reliable, understandable systems.

## Commercial Objective
Generate service revenue while developing reusable technical assets, knowledge, integrations, and product hypotheses that can compound over time.

## Non-Goals
- Do not build large speculative systems before validating pain.
- Do not add AI merely for novelty.
- Do not accept architecture complexity without a business reason.
- Do not claim enterprise-grade security, compliance, scale, or reliability without evidence.

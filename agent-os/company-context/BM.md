# BM Visuals — Company Context

## Role
BM Visuals is the front-of-house visual and web service arm. It helps clients improve what customers see, feel, and interact with.

## Current Stage
BUILD / VALIDATE. Priority is to establish trust, portfolio proof, repeatable offers, and early revenue before scaling.

## Core Offers
- Brand and marketing websites
- Landing pages
- E-commerce / Shopify / WordPress implementation where appropriate
- UI/UX design and implementation
- Conversion and booking flows
- Lead capture
- SEO-ready website foundations
- Basic customer-facing automation when it improves the experience

## Early ICP
Prioritize businesses where aesthetics and digital trust materially affect buying decisions:
- Fashion and apparel
- Accessories and jewelry
- Beauty, spa, skincare, wellness
- Hospitality and lifestyle businesses
- Young modern SMEs seeking a stronger digital identity or refresh

Geography may include Vietnam local brands and international SMBs. Do not assume one geography is permanently primary without current evidence.

## Positioning Direction
BM should not compete as a generic cheap web builder. The intended territory is:

**Aesthetic quality × interaction quality × conversion clarity × professional delivery.**

The website should feel distinctive while remaining useful, fast, responsive, and commercially clear.

## Current Proof Assets
Existing and developing demo/case-study directions include export/business, spa, coffee, dental, company/corporate, personal branding, skincare, and future fashion work.

## Brand Experience
Desired feel: modern, intentional, polished, fluid, editorial, and memorable. Motion and micro-interactions should support narrative and usability rather than exist as decoration.

## Commercial Objective
Use strong proof and targeted outreach to close initial projects, build case studies, create trust, and finance further development.

## Non-Goals
- Do not position BM as a full-service agency for every possible service.
- Do not promise capabilities not demonstrated or deliverable by the team.
- Do not sacrifice clarity or conversion for visual gimmicks.
- Do not treat demo websites as fabricated client outcomes.

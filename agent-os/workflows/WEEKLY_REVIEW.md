# Workflow: Weekly Founder Review

## Objective
Give the Founder one compact operating view across BM/BMP and turn scattered activity into decisions.

## Inputs
- BM sales activity and pipeline
- BM build/portfolio status
- BMP product/service experiments
- Delivery blockers
- Cash/tool spend if available
- Important market/competitor signals

## Flow
1. Orchestrator collects current state from relevant owners/agents.
2. Market Intelligence summarizes meaningful external changes only.
3. Outbound Strategist summarizes pipeline learning, not just activity volume.
4. Evidence QA surfaces unresolved quality risks.
5. Finance Analyst reports material cash/cost changes when data exists.
6. Orchestrator produces the Founder Review.

## Founder Review Format
### 1. Executive State
- BM stage/status
- BMP stage/status
- top 3 outcomes this week

### 2. Revenue
- leads researched
- meaningful outbound sent
- replies / positive replies
- meetings / qualified opportunities
- proposals / wins / losses
- key learning

### 3. Product and Delivery
- shipped
- in progress
- blocked
- QA failures requiring attention

### 4. Market Intelligence
- new customer/competitor signal
- implication
- action, if any

### 5. Finance
- cash-impacting changes
- unusual spend
- upcoming commitment
- runway only when reliable data exists

### 6. Decisions Required
Each decision includes options, recommendation, tradeoff, and deadline/trigger if relevant.

### 7. Next Week
Maximum 3 company-level priorities with owner and measurable outcome.

## Rule
Do not fill missing data with guesses. Mark it UNKNOWN and identify the smallest action needed to obtain it.

# Workflow: BMP Product / Productized Service Discovery

## Objective
Identify B2B pains worth validating before BMP invests in a product, reusable system, or productized service.

## Flow
1. Founder defines a market/problem area or asks for opportunity discovery.
2. **Market Intelligence** collects evidence of recurring pain, current workaround, problem owner, urgency, WTP signals, accessibility, competitors/substitutes, and distribution options.
3. Score each opportunity on pain, frequency, urgency, WTP, accessibility, build speed, distribution advantage, retention, team fit, and moat potential.
4. Select one narrow hypothesis, not a broad platform idea.
5. Define the smallest validation artifact: interviews, manual service, audit, clickable prototype, technical spike, landing page, or paid pilot.
6. Founder approves whether to spend build capacity.
7. Orchestrator converts the approved validation into tasks and owners.
8. Evidence QA validates implementation outputs when software/prototypes are produced.
9. Record what was learned and decide: CONTINUE / PIVOT / PAUSE / KILL.

## Opportunity Brief
- Customer/ICP
- Pain
- Trigger
- Current workaround
- Why current alternatives are insufficient
- Problem owner / buyer
- WTP evidence
- Reachability/distribution
- Competitors/substitutes
- Proposed mechanism
- Why BMP may win
- Validation experiment
- Cost/time constraints
- Success/failure threshold
- Major unknowns

## Anti-Patterns
- Building because technology is interesting.
- Calling a generic chatbot a differentiated product.
- Starting with a large dashboard/platform before proving the workflow pain.
- Using TAM as a substitute for reachable demand.
- Treating positive feedback as payment intent.

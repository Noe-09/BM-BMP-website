# Workflow: BM Sales

## Objective
Turn a defined ICP into a small number of well-researched, high-fit opportunities and learn from every response.

## Flow
1. **Market Intelligence** selects/validates segment, account fit criteria, disqualifiers, and buying/redesign signals.
2. Build a target account batch small enough for real research.
3. For each account, collect observable trigger, website/social state, likely decision maker, pain hypothesis, and relevant proof asset.
4. **Outbound Strategist** produces the outreach angle and multi-touch sequence.
5. Founder approves external messaging/sends unless explicit authority exists.
6. Log outcomes: no response, negative, neutral, positive, meeting, qualified opportunity.
7. Feed objections and repeated pains back to Market Intelligence and offer/positioning decisions.

## Minimum Account Record
- company/brand
- segment
- geography
- website/social URLs
- fit score
- trigger/evidence
- decision maker/contact
- pain hypothesis
- relevant BM demo/proof
- message angle
- status
- last touch
- next action
- learning

## Rules
- Quality over volume during validation.
- Never describe a demo as paid client work if it is not.
- No invented traffic, revenue, conversion, or business impact claims.
- Stop or adjust sequences when evidence shows poor fit.

## Weekly Learning Loop
Review which segments, triggers, problems, proof assets, and CTAs generate positive replies. Update ICP and outreach rules from evidence rather than intuition.

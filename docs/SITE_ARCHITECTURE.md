# BM Visuals — Site Architecture

## Primary Goal

Build a focused creative studio website that:

1. establishes credibility
2. communicates BM's positioning
3. showcases selected work
4. demonstrates digital craft
5. creates qualified project inquiries

---

# Primary Routes

## /

Homepage

Purpose:

- establish brand perception
- explain BM quickly
- showcase flagship work
- demonstrate capability
- direct visitors toward projects or contact

---

## /work

All Work

Purpose:

- portfolio archive
- allow visitors to explore more projects
- show range without overcrowding homepage

Possible filters later:

- All
- Fashion
- Beauty
- Hospitality
- Business
- Experimental

Filters are optional for V1.

---

## /work/[slug]

Individual project case study.

Purpose:

- transform project screenshots into professional case studies
- show thinking and craft
- create higher perceived value

---

## /studio

Studio / About

Purpose:

- explain BM philosophy
- describe capabilities
- introduce team
- establish how BM works
- reinforce trust

---

## /lab

BM Lab

Purpose:

- experimental work
- self-initiated projects
- creative coding
- R&D
- visual studies

This route is lower priority than Homepage and Work.

---

## /contact

Project Inquiry

Purpose:

- provide a low-friction way to start a conversation
- capture qualified project information

---

# Homepage Architecture

## 01 — Global Navigation

Suggested:

BM Visuals

Work
Studio
Lab
Start a Project

Navigation should remain visually minimal.

---

# 02 — Hero

Goal:

Create immediate visual impact.

Communicate that BM is not a generic agency.

Working copy:

Independent Digital Studio

We create digital identities
people remember.

Websites, digital experiences and creative systems
built around ambitious brands.

Primary CTA:

View Selected Work

Secondary CTA:

Start a Project

Hero may include a signature interactive visual.

Do not let the visual overpower the message.

---

# 03 — Positioning Statement

Purpose:

Explain BM in one strong statement.

Possible copy:

Brands shouldn't just exist online.

They should feel alive.

Supporting text:

BM Visuals combines strategy, art direction,
UI/UX, motion and development to create
digital experiences built around the identity
of each brand.

---

# 04 — Selected Work

Most important homepage section.

Show 3–4 flagship projects.

Initial order:

01 Fashion Project
02 Aurelia Skin
03 Personal Branding
04 Experimental / Creative Project

Each project should display:

- number
- project title
- category
- year
- descriptor
- media
- CTA

Avoid generic card grids.

---

# 05 — Philosophy Statement

Purpose:

Differentiate BM from template agencies.

Possible statement:

Not another website.

A digital experience built around your brand.

Supporting statement:

We do not start with templates.

We begin with identity, audience and experience.

---

# 06 — Capabilities

Keep concise.

Suggested categories:

STRATEGY

- Digital Direction
- Experience Strategy
- Brand Research
- Creative Direction

DESIGN

- Art Direction
- UI/UX
- Visual Systems
- Interactive Design

MOTION

- Motion Direction
- Micro-interactions
- Digital Storytelling
- 3D where appropriate

BUILD

- Creative Development
- Frontend Development
- Ecommerce
- CMS Integration

Do not turn this into a large service catalog.

---

# 07 — BM Lab Preview

Purpose:

2–3 experimental pieces.

CTA:

# 08 — Studio Statement

Small team.

BM Visuals is an independent multidisciplinary
studio working across design, motion and technology.

Keep team presentation concise.
